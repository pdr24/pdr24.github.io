Data Pruning Across Learning Tasks

Undergraduate Honors Thesis

Investigated whether data pruning methods designed to identify high-utility samples for image classification transfer effectively to semantic segmentation. Evaluated pruning strategies across segmentation models and datasets to characterize how sample-utility signals generalize across learning tasks and their potential for reducing training data requirements.

Data-Efficient ML Data Pruning Semantic Segmentation

Scientific Image Semantic Segmentation for Materials Defect Detection

MM4Mat Workshop Spotlight Paper @ CVPR 2025 · Research Posters

Developed a semantic segmentation codebase using Meta's Segment Anything 2 (SAM2) and adapted promptable segmentation for domain-specific materials datasets. Evaluated SAM2, DeepLabV3, PRNet, and few-shot segmentation approaches to investigate methods for accurately localizing material defects when labeled scientific imaging data is limited.

Distributed DNN Inference on Edge Devices

IEEE EdgeCom 2025 Paper · Research Posters

Co-developed a distributed inference pipeline using PyTorch RPC to partition DNN execution across resource-constrained Raspberry Pi edge devices. Benchmarked multiple CNN architectures and profiled inference latency, throughput, CPU utilization, and memory usage to characterize the performance and resource tradeoffs of distributed deep learning at the edge.

Systems & Networks (SyN) Lab

Advisor: Dr. Rajendra Boppana

Research Areas: Machine Learning, Network Security, Malware Detection.

Out-of-Distribution Generalization for IoT Malware Detection

RSAC Security Scholar Poster Exhibition · Research Posters

Developed a configurable machine learning experimentation pipeline to investigate out-of-distribution generalization in network-based IoT malware detection. Systematically varied classifiers, feature configurations, and training and testing datasets to characterize performance under cross-dataset distribution shifts using IoT-23, ToN-IoT, and BoT-IoT data. Later explored LLM-based custom feature generation from network traffic as an alternative to commonly defined features.

OOD Generalization IoT Security Machine Learning

Simulated Stressed System Data for Ransomware Detection

Research Poster

Investigated whether benign resource-intensive workloads could simulate system behavior useful for training machine learning models for ransomware detection without directly executing ransomware. Generated stressed system data and evaluated whether resource-utilization patterns could be distinguished from normal benign activity, exploring a safer approach to collecting data for malware-detection research.

Interactive Tools for K-12 AI Education

ACM ITiCSE 2025 · IEEE ISEC 2025 · ACM SIGCSE TS 2026

Developed interactive web-based learning tools that expose the internal mechanics of AI and computing concepts to middle school students: RuleRunners for rule-based AI, AI-See for image recognition, IntoTheRabbitHole for search algorithms, and FaunaForest for decision trees. Evaluated the tools with middle school students in an afterschool AI program to study how interactive, challenge-based activities can support understanding of otherwise abstract computational concepts.

AI Literacy Computing Education Interactive Learning K-12

DoppelBot — Identifying LLM Agents in Collaborative Settings

EMNLP 2026 Main Conference Paper

Investigated how middle school students identify LLM agents in live collaborative settings using interaction data collected through DoppelBot. Analyzed and annotated student interactions to characterize the reasoning strategies students use to distinguish AI agents from human collaborators, contributing a CS education perspective to the study.
