RuleRunners: A Two-Player Collaborative Software Tool for Introducing Rule-Based AI Systems to Middle School Students

Authors: [List authors in publication order]

AI Education Rule-Based AI Collaborative Learning

Overview

RuleRunners is a two-player collaborative learning tool designed to introduce middle school students to rule-based artificial intelligence. Through interactive gameplay, students work with rules and observe how rule-based systems make decisions, supporting hands-on exploration of foundational AI concepts.

About the Presentation

This presentation introduces the motivation behind RuleRunners, explains the learning experience, and walks through the game's levels to illustrate how students interact with rule-based AI concepts.
