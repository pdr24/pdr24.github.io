FaunaForest: A Novel Software Tool for Teaching Decision Trees to Middle School Students

Authors: Pragathi Durga Rajarajan, Adrian Cisneros, Dr. Fred Martin

University of Texas at San Antonio - Engaging Computing Group

K-12 AI Education Decision Trees Interactive Learning Tools

About

FaunaForest was created by Pragathi Durga Rajarajan and Adrian Cisneros as part of the Developing AI Tools for K-12 course at the University of Texas at San Antonio, under the guidance of Dr. Fred Martin. A full paper was published at the IEEE Integrated STEM Education Conference (2025).

FaunaForest aims to teach middle school students (grades 6-8) about decision trees through three levels of interactive decision tree puzzles. Each puzzle involves completing a decision tree with blank mystery nodes so that it correctly classifies various animals.
