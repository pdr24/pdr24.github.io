IntoTheRabbitHole: A Web Application for Teaching Middle School Students About Search Algorithms

Authors: Pragathi Durga Rajarajan, Dr. Fred Martin

University of Texas at San Antonio - Engaging Computing Group

K-12 AI Education Interactive Learning Tools

About

I developed IntoTheRabbitHole, an interactive web application designed to introduce middle school students (grades 6–8) to fundamental search algorithms, as part of the Developing AI Tools for K-12 project at the University of Texas at San Antonio, under the guidance of Dr. Fred Martin. The project focuses on making abstract search concepts more accessible through interactive, puzzle-based learning. This work was published at the ACM Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education (ITiCSE 2025) conference.

IntoTheRabbitHole features dedicated training puzzles for depth-first search (DFS) and breadth-first search (BFS), followed by a timed challenge level. In each puzzle, students navigate a graph to help a rabbit find its carrot, applying search strategies to reach the goal. IntoTheRabbitHole is designed to help students visualize graph traversal, explore how different search algorithms operate, and develop a more intuitive understanding of fundamental AI search techniques.
