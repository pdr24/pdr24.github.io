RuleRunners: A Two-Player Collaborative Software Tool for Introducing Rule-Based AI Systems to Middle School Students

Authors: Pragathi Durga Rajarajan, Dr. Fred Martin

University of Texas at San Antonio - Engaging Computing Group

K-12 AI Education Rule-Based AI Interactive Learning Tools Collaborative Learning

About

I developed RuleRunners, an interactive, two-player web application designed to introduce middle school students to rule-based artificial intelligence through a parkour-style game. The project focuses on making the underlying logic of rule-based AI systems more accessible through hands-on experimentation, collaborative learning, and friendly competition. This work was accepted for publication at ACM SIGCSE Virtual 2026, co-authored with Dr. Fred Martin.

RuleRunners allows two students to share a single device as they explore the fundamentals of rule-based systems, including conditions, actions, and rule execution. Students can construct their own sets of rules to control an AI parkour agent, observing how their choices influence the agent's behavior as it navigates the game. The experience combines opportunities for collaboration and competition, encouraging students to experiment with different rule configurations and reason about how explicit rules determine an AI system's actions. Through this approach, I aimed to make symbolic AI concepts more tangible and engaging for younger learners.
