On Teaching Image Recognition to Children at a Summer Camp

Authors: Pragathi Durga Rajarajan, Dr. Fred Martin

University of Texas at San Antonio - Engaging Computing Group

K-12 AI Education Image Recognition AI-See

About

I developed and delivered an image recognition learning module as part of the University of Texas at San Antonio's AI for Everyone summer camp (2025), under the guidance of Dr. Fred Martin. Designed to introduce middle school students (grades 6–8) to foundational concepts in computer vision, the module focuses on making the underlying processes of image recognition accessible through hands-on and interactive activities. This work was published at the ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE TS 2026).



The module combines an unplugged Make-A-Matrix activity, AI-See (a novel interactive software tool for exploring feature maps and kernel operations), DoodleIt , and group discussions throughout. Through these activities, I aimed to help students understand how mathematical operations can be applied to images to extract features, making the computational concepts underlying image recognition systems more tangible and approachable.
